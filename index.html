About Me

I'm Ahmed Abdelaziz Mohamed Elngar — a backend architect based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, working mainly in Laravel and PHP.

Over the past four years I've built and maintained the kind of systems businesses actually run on: ERP platforms handling customers, inventory and invoicing; real-time vehicle tracking with live dashboards and WASL synchronization; pilgrimage logistics for Umra operators; and multi-tenant SaaS products serving several clients from a single codebase.

A lot of that work lives at the integration layer, where things are least forgiving. I've implemented ZATCA e-invoice compliance for Saudi tax reporting, wired up payment gateways including Noon Payments and Bookeey end to end — checkout, callbacks and reconciliation — and delivered course video over HLS with protected playback so paid content stays paid.

Day to day I work across the whole backend: schema design in MySQL, REST APIs consumed by both web and mobile clients, background jobs and queues, and deployment on AWS. Code quality matters to me — at AlBadr I worked under SonarQube quality gates, and I lean on clean, maintainable architecture over clever shortcuts.

What I enjoy most is being involved before the first migration is written. At Idara.net and across my freelance work I help shape the business idea itself — scoping what should be built, questioning requirements that won't survive contact with real users, and turning rough concepts into systems that hold up in production.

At Idara.net that now extends to delivery: I plan upcoming features, break them into tasks and hand them out across the team, then review what comes back and confirm each feature actually meets its requirements before it ships. I also work outside the backend when a product needs it — I built the Flutter mobile app for Ilzam alongside its Laravel platform.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Mansoura University, and I'm open to backend, architecture and consulting work.
